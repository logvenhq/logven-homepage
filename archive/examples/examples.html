Work that runs on Logven

  • Reliability engineering

    {#- -#}

    Vibration trend per engine

    Fits a trend line to hot-fire vibration readings and opens a reliability action when a test exceeds 4.8 mm/s.

    {#- -#} After each test

  • Test engineering

    {#- -#}

    Telemetry anomaly screen

    Runs a rolling z-score over 50 Hz sensor channels and flags excursions beyond 3σ, tagged with run ID and channel.

    {#- -#} On new telemetry file

  • Quality

    {#- -#}

    NCR from inspection photos

    Reads gauge photos with a vision model, checks them against drawing tolerances and drafts the non-conformance report.

    {#- -#} On new inspection

  • Procurement

    {#- -#}

    Lead-time drift

    Parses supplier confirmations, updates promised dates and flags parts that push the build plan past its gate.

    {#- -#} On new PDF

  • Process engineering

    {#- -#}

    SPC control limits

    Recomputes X̄–R control limits per line and alerts on Western Electric rule violations with the offending samples.

    {#- -#} Every shift

    {#- -#} Monthly

  • Maintenance

    {#- -#}

    {#- -#} Nightly

    {#- -#} Every 15 minutes
