# Model marks

These marks identify the model families used by Logven. They remain the property of their respective owners.

- `gemma.png`: https://ai.google.dev/gemma/images/gemma_sq.png, linked from https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs.
- `mistral.svg`: unmodified RGB gradient icon from the official 2026 logo collection at https://mistral.ai/cms-media/api/documents/file/Mistral_Logos_2026.zip, linked from https://mistral.ai/brand/.
- `liquid-ai.svg`: https://www.liquid.ai/logos/liquid-ai-black.svg. The SVG is used as a mask to display a black or white wordmark for the current theme.
- `ibm.svg` and `ibm-dark.svg`: https://github.com/carbon-design-system/carbon-for-ibm-dotcom/tree/main/packages/styles/icons/svg (`IBM-8bar-logo--h23.svg` and `IBM-8bar-logo--h23-white.svg`). The light version sets the path fill to IBM's black (`#161616`); the source expects a fill supplied by its host stylesheet.
- `nvidia.svg`: the green eye mark from the official header SVG at https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/foundation-models/nemotron/. The path and brand color are preserved, with a viewBox fitted to the mark.

Family capability references: https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs, https://docs.mistral.ai/models, https://www.liquid.ai/models, https://www.ibm.com/granite, https://developer.nvidia.com/topics/ai/Nemotron. Tile captions describe family capabilities rather than individual model versions or automatic routing behavior.

- `gemma-spark.svg`: a solid four-point sparkle in Gemma's gradient stops (#446EFF, #2E96FF, #B1C5FF), drawn for this site. It is not an official Google file; swap it for the official solid mark once one is available.
- `liquid-ai-drop.svg`: the drop paths from `liquid-ai.svg`, without the wordmark, with the viewBox fitted to the drop.
