Brand partnerships

When the Logven lockup appears next to a partner’s logo, separate the two with a thin vertical rule and give them the same visual weight. Keep the clear space around each logo, show each one at a comfortable size in its approved colours, and don’t distort or alter either of them.

Both companies approve co-branded material before it’s published. Send drafts to info@logven.com.
