The operating model of your company, built from the work itself.

Logven lets your teams model their processes and automate them, on small private models that run on your own hardware. From that work it builds an operating model of the company, and keeps it as the system of record management can rely on.

Thesis

Every company runs on processes nobody has written down. Logven writes them down by running them.

The idea

A company is state plus processes. State is its data. Processes are what act on that data. Logven makes both explicit, in one structured, versioned system.

The model comes from the bottom up. Nobody draws it in a workshop or maintains it by hand. Teams model and automate the processes they already run, and every one of them becomes part of a single operating model of the company.

Once the company is computable, you can ask it questions. Emails, documents, figures and records all land as data in the same model. Instead of chasing people for status, management can ask the system and get answers grounded in the data.

The problem

Specialised teams spend their days on manual work around data. They pull values out of PDFs, update spreadsheets and databases, consolidate and analyse, write reports, handle responses and chase anomalies.

That knowledge lives in people’s heads and scattered files. Management has no reliable picture of how the work actually gets done, so it can’t standardise it, scale it or improve it. Process maps go stale the week they’re drawn, and custom software takes a year and breaks the first time the process changes.

How it works

Reliable by design. Adaptive by nature. Logven uses AI to build deterministic automations, so you get the flexibility of language models and the predictability of software.

1. Model. Teams describe and run their processes in Logven, working with it the way they would with an assistant. Each process gets a clear structure: its inputs, its steps, its outputs.

2. Automate. Logven turns those processes into automations that run the same way every time, with approvals at the decision points that matter. Language models are used only where there is real ambiguity.

3. Housekeeping. Every night, Logven reviews what ran. It merges duplicate processes, aligns data into shared models, fixes drift and standardises where it makes sense. The system stays clean on its own, so it doesn’t decay into another tangle of scripts.

4. Operating model. Every process and every piece of data is connected into one model of how the company runs. It’s the system of record for operations, the way CRM is for customers and ERP is for money.

Private by default. Logven runs on small models on your own hardware. Your data and your processes never leave the building.

An example

We explored this with a public-sector geothermal team.

The team manages data from 36 geothermal plants: extracting values from PDFs, updating spreadsheets and databases, consolidating and analysing, producing reports, processing responses and resolving anomalies.

The result is a plant-operations system that emerged from the team’s own work, not from a specification written upfront. It is reproducible, easy to maintain, and relies on models less over time, which keeps running costs down.

What makes it different

Process tools describe the company. Logven runs it.

Process mapping and mining show you how work happened. Logven is where the work happens, so improving the model improves the work.

AI agent tools are flexible but unpredictable. Logven’s automations are deterministic, traceable and reproducible.

Custom software needs a spec upfront and decays as processes change. Logven is built bottom-up from real work and maintains itself through housekeeping.

Cloud AI sends your data out. Logven runs on private models on your hardware.

One system, from the work of a single team to the model of the whole company.

Who it’s for

Logven is built for enterprise and medium-to-large companies, for the people who do the work and the people accountable for it.

Technical, specialised teams get the repetitive work off their plate, and their expertise kept as processes that run reliably. VPs and department heads get a clear, up-to-date picture of how their department works, and a system they can standardise and improve.
