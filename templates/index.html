Centralise your teams’ data work.

Your teams organise their data work their own way. Logven turns it into one central data lake, keeps it tidy and shows you all the data you have, even the data you didn’t know about.

Start a pilot How it works

Before every decision, someone spends days gathering and analysing data

It’s spread across people, files, spreadsheets and emails, and nobody can see how it fits together. Most companies don’t even know what data they already have. Once it’s found, someone still has to clean it, combine it and work through it in spreadsheets.

Search tools and AI assistants find pieces of it, but you can’t rely on the answers. Data platforms only work once someone has shaped the data and loaded it by hand.

  • Spreadsheet
  • Email thread
  • PDF report
  • Database export

Give your teams simple tools for their own data work

Each team gathers, models and analyses its data the way it already works, minus the copying, pasting and chasing. The repetitive parts run on their own, so the work moves smoothly from one week to the next.

There’s no data project to start first and no month-long setup. Each team picks it up and gets going.

One place for all your data

Structured data from your spreadsheets, databases and the systems your teams already use. Unstructured data from reports, emails, PDFs and photos. Logven brings both into one central data lake, clean and connected, without anyone loading it by hand.

Every source comes in, structured or not

Each team builds pipelines for the data it works with, from spreadsheets to sensor readings. Logven brings it all together in one place, linked and ready for analysis.

Every app and file

Workers let people build their own data pipelines from the systems and files they use. If a connection is missing, we build it.

Structured or not

Tables, forms, emails, PDFs, scans and sensor readings all go in. Models read the documents, and code checks every field they return.

One record, not three versions

The customer that sales, finance and support each keep becomes one record, linked to every order, invoice and email behind it.

Always current

Logven syncs every source on a schedule, so you always see today’s data.

It stays tidy, and it gets better as it grows

Data lakes rarely get messy all at once. A sheet ends up in the wrong process, two teams keep the same list, a worker stops being used. Every night Logven looks over the whole workspace, tells you what to tidy and spots the work your teams keep repeating. Nothing changes until you accept it.

Everything in its place

Sheets that work together belong in the same process. Logven spots the ones that sit apart and suggests where they fit.

One copy of everything

When two teams keep the same list, Logven finds both and suggests merging them into one.

Nothing forgotten

A worker that hasn’t run in a week, a board nobody opens, a sheet whose data goes nowhere: each one is flagged.

Repeated work, spotted

Steps your team repeats every week come back as a suggested automation. Approve it, and it runs as code.

Your data stays yours

Logven runs on your own servers, or in the cloud with European data residency. It uses small, private AI models, so your data never leaves your control.

  • Language.Reads and writes text.

    Language models for documents and email

    They read and write emails, reports and specs. Nothing gets sent to an outside service.

  • Vision.Turns images into data.

    Vision models for drawings, forms and photos

    They read scanned forms, technical drawings and inspection photos right where those files are stored.

  • Time series.Forecasts what comes next.

    Time-series models for sensor data

    They forecast readings and spot anomalies, and your telemetry never leaves your network.

  • Decisions.Weighs up the options.

    Decision models for planning

  • Retrieval.Finds what you need.

    Retrieval models for company knowledge

Every automation is written as code

It does what you approved, every time.

Your team describes the task and Logven drafts the code. Someone approves it, and from then on it runs the same way.

It follows the approved steps.
The same rules run in the same order on every run.
It only sees what it needs.
Each worker gets access to specific data and tools, and nothing else.
Every run leaves a record.
Inputs, outputs and calls to other systems are logged. You can trace any result back to the step that produced it.
Changes need a sign-off.
Every change is a new version, and it can’t run until someone approves it.

Some steps need a model, and the code keeps it in check.

Reading a supplier’s PDF is a good example. The code asks the model, checks the answer and decides what to do with it.

A model gets one step.
It never runs the whole process.
Answers get checked.
If an answer comes back in the wrong shape, the code throws it out.
The rules live in code.
Calculations, business rules and the order of steps are never handed to a model.
Every call is saved.
Each request and its answer are stored with the run.

Teams, never individuals. Groups under five are combined.

Every dataset, one place

Patterns and activity

Seasonal peaks, slowdowns and the handovers where work waits, spotted before they turn into problems.

Counted by team
