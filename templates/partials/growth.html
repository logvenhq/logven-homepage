1. 01First week

    Describe

    Begin with a task in the team’s own words. Make the inputs, steps and expected result explicit.

  2. 02First month

    Connect

    Bring the information behind the task into a shared state, with clear relationships between the facts.

  3. 03First quarter

    Formalise

    Express repeatable steps as functions. Keep the rules explicit, and use models where interpretation is needed.

  4. 04First year

    Model

    Connect state and functions across teams to reveal how your operations work together.

The model is refined as the work changes: shared logic is reused, overlapping processes are brought together, and obsolete steps are retired.
