5.2 Acceptance limits

CharacteristicLimitSensor
Peak chamber pressure975 – 990 kPaPT-1, PT-2
Vibration, RMS≤ 10 mm/sACC-1
Vibration, peak≤ 11 mm/sACC-1, ACC-2
Burn duration395 – 420 sSequencer

A test is accepted when every characteristic falls within its limit. A result outside a limit is recorded as an anomaly, and the engine is held until the review board signs off a retest.

5.3 Retests

A retest uses the same engine and sequence, reported as R1, R2 and so on after the original test reference.
